    How to Use Video Subtitle
    1. Add the provided subtitle script to your website.
    2. Allow the system to scan your website and automatically discover all supported videos.
    3. Decorative videos are detected automatically and marked as decorative by default. You can change this at any time using the toggle.
    4. Select the videos you want to remediate, then choose whether you want Single Language or Multiple Languages subtitles.
    5. Wait for remediation to complete.
    6. Open any remediated video on your website and play it. Subtitles will be displayed automatically in your website's default language.
    Note:
    • Multi-language subtitles are not available with the standalone Video Subtitle plan. To enable multilingual subtitles, you must also purchase the All in One Accessibility Widget.
    • Duplicate videos are detected automatically and do not require separate remediation.
    • Supported video players: YouTube, Native HTML5 Player, and Video.js. Support for additional players is coming soon.
